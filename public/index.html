How to Install on Android Phone
1
Tap "Download Android App (.apk)"

Your browser will save `LifePro-AI.apk` into your Downloads folder.

2
Allow "Install from Unknown Sources"

When opening the APK, tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source" (standard for direct GitHub APKs).

3
Tap Install & Open

Launch Life Pro.AI. Grant microphone & emergency permissions to enable voice safety protection.
